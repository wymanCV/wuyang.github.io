Staff Research Engineer · Ant Group

Wuyang LI

I am a Staff Research Engineer at Ant Group (June 16, 2026 – Present).

I build stable and trustworthy multimodal intelligence that connects perception and generation across domains, modalities, and real-world settings. My research spans open-world and 3D perception, video and world generation, and AI for science, with the long-term goal of enabling general-purpose AI for digital life, autonomy, healthcare, and scientific discovery. My own and supervised projects cover the following research directions:

  • AI for Digital Twins: Digital twins, digital humans, video generative models, 3D spatial reasoning
  • AI for Autonomous Mobility: 3D Occupancy Prediction, Open-vocabulary/Cross-domain/Open-set Object Detection
  • AI for Scientific Innovation: Nanophotonics, meta-optics, graph-based learning, Computational Photography
  • AI for Transforming Medicine: Brain MRI Analysis, Medical Report Generation, 4D Surgical Simulation

Before joining Ant Group, I was a Research Scientist at the Visual Intelligence for Transportation Lab (VITA), École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL), where I worked with Prof. Alexandre Alahi. Before that, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher (2024–2025) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK). I completed my Ph.D. (2020–2023) at the City University of Hong Kong (CityUHK) with early graduation, supervised by Prof. Yixuan Yuan. During my Ph.D., I focused on visual perception for autonomous driving, addressing challenges caused by out-of-distribution data and domain shifts. I also had the opportunity to work with Prof. Bo Han. I completed my undergraduate studies at Tianjin University (2016–2020).
